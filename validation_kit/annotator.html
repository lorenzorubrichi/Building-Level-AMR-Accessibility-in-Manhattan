Entrance accessibility – blind annotation

Load the task list and the image folder to start.

Q0 · Can the entrance of the target building be judged? Target = building facing the camera at the image centre.

If no, why?

Q1 · Stairs or steps between sidewalk and door? Any step up or down, including a single step or a stoop.

Q2 · Gate, fence or barrier in front of the entrance? Gate/fence/railing that must be opened or passed through to reach the door.

Q3 · Ramp or step-free sloped access visible?

Q4 · Could a sidewalk delivery robot reach the door area? Robot rolls on flat ground, cannot climb steps, cannot open gates or doors.

Rules
