Definitions
  • Gate: a gate, fence or barrier that must be opened or passed through to reach the door. A railing along stairs or around a basement areaway is not a gate if the path to the door is open.
  • Ramp: a ramp or step-free sloped access to the entrance. A sloping sidewalk is not a ramp.
